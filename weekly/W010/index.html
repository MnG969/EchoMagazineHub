Are They Hacking Freezers Now?

Are freezers across Europe really being cyber-attacked?

BY AHMET TUĞRA HİSAR 2 MIN READ

Welcome our dear readers. Today I will be clarifying a new controversy that is taking place in Europe. As you can see from the title, freezers across Europe are getting cyber-attacked. In October, freezers around France malfunctioned, which the government called it a cyber-attack.

But recently, again, retailers from at least seven more countries in Europe has reported problems with their freezing units. Then, after a week, the French government calls out Iran for these refrigerator malfunctions.

When we look more into the situation, we see the same type of malfunctions over the summer in The U.S. Military. According to a Facebook post, all of the freezers went into defrost mode and the whole food was spoiled. The same day, a Naval Station stated that they had some problems with the cold chain.

As the government was looking into this, the Pentagon acknowledged ‘’speculation regarding a potential cyber-incident’’ and that they were investigating it.

Which Countries Were Affected?

The countries that were also targeted are France, Belgium, Estonia, Finland, Ireland, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, and Sweden. Even those who were behind these attacks, French prosecutors have blamed Iran for the widespread malfunctioning of the cold chain machines.

Can Freezers Really Be Hacked?

And if you are asking: Can these machines even get hacked? The answer is yes, many supermarkets and large chains use centrally connected refrigeration systems with temperatures that are set remotely using internet connections.

So that was all. Thanks for reading!
