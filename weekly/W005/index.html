Blood

What is most loved, always found covered with mud.

BY VESPERA 1 MIN READ

What is most loved,
Always found covered with mud,
Scud through the night,
Glowes in the dark,

Oh Lord my Lord,
My soul or his blood?
What is this taste I have in my gut?
Thought hit my heart on the ground

What is this taste I have in my gut?
My soul or his blood?

-Vespera
